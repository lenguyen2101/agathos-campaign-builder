Who can do what: roles and permissions

Following the team's answers of 2 October 2026, v1.2 merges the roles into one list for organisations and projects: Owner, Manager and Viewer. This page shows what each role can do in the prototype, what is still open, and the answers it is based on.

Sources: team answers, 2 Oct 2026 · handoff, Diagram 1c · flow spec, Step 5 and Step 10 · prototype at /onboarding

One list of roles

Owner

Manager

What changed from v1.1

Admin and Collaborator are now Manager

The team suggested combining them (Q5). One role, with a name that isn't "Admin".

Member is gone

So is the screen where a member asked an owner for permission. A request to join remains only for a new user whose organisation is already verified.

Project roles follow the same list

Editor and Withdrawal-authorised are replaced by Manager, plus a payout permission switch. Viewer stays.

Still open

The name "Manager"

Proposed in v1.2. Needs the team's OK.

Q2

What only an Owner can do

The team asked to follow the live platform. We need its rules to fill in that row.

Q3

A separate payout role

The team is still deciding. The prototype uses a per-person switch for now.

Q4

Who approves a request to join

The prototype notifies the organisation's primary contact. There's no rule for what happens if nobody answers.

Withdrawal rights are still asked in two places

Individuals still name who can request withdrawals under Contact & authority, while the Team step has payout permission. Organizations follow the live form, which doesn't ask this. Partnerships is deciding whether the question is needed.
