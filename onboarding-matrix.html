Who can do what: roles and permissions

The feedback on "Selecting Org as Collaborator" asked for a review of roles. This page lists every role in the onboarding prototype, what each one can do today, where the rules conflict, and the questions to settle. Nothing here is final. The prototype's behaviour is a placeholder until the team decides.

Sources: handoff, Diagram 1c · flow spec, Step 5 and Step 10 · prototype at /onboarding · change-request PRD (admin portal)

Two sets of roles, plus Agathos staff

Organisation roles
OwnerAdminCollaboratorMember

One per person per organisation. Decides what they see when they start a project. The handoff names Owner, Admin and Collaborator. "Member" is the prototype's name for the handoff's "anything else".

Project roles
EditorViewerWithdrawal-authorised

Given per project in the Team step, by email invite. Straight from flow spec Step 10.

Agathos staff
Agathos admin

Works in the admin portal. Approves verification and change requests on live projects. Not a user role, but it shares the word "Admin".

Individual fundraisers
One person

No organisation, so no roles. The individual does everything, and can invite project roles like anyone else.

Where the rules conflict

"Admin" means two different people

An organisation Admin is a user on the charity's side. An Agathos admin is our staff member who approves change requests. On a screen that says "ask an admin", users can't tell which one.

A collaborator can hand out payout rights

The collaborator screen says payouts and team access belong to the owner. But in the Team step, a collaborator can invite anyone as Withdrawal-authorised.

Collaborator screen · Team step

Withdrawal rights are set in two places

Contact & authority asks for names in free text during verification. The Team step gives the Withdrawal-authorised role by email. A hint asks for them to match, but nothing checks it. Partnerships is already deciding whether the first field is needed at all.

Contact & authority · Team step · feedback item on withdrawal POC

No one clearly approves access requests

A member is told to "ask an owner or admin", but the prototype only notifies the organisation's primary contact. There's no rule for what happens if that person never answers.

Request-access screen

Owner and Admin behave the same

Every check in the prototype treats them as equal. If they are equal, one of them isn't needed. If they differ, the difference isn't written down anywhere.

Collaborators see an extra screen

The handoff sends a collaborator straight to project creation. The prototype shows a screen with a notice and a Start button first. This is the screen in the feedback.

Handoff, Diagram 1c · collaborator screen
