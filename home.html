Raising funds for your initiative?

Let’s get in touch →

Seeking to be compliant with the prevailing regulations relating to charitable giving, agathos enables organisations and individuals to start and manage projects safely, effectively and with ease of mind.

Our team will provide personalised support to guide you in setting up your project and find the best arrangement to meet your specific needs.

  • Updates Feature: Keep all of your supporters up to date with our Updates feature. Seek prayer coverage from those invested in your cause and allow them to journey with you. You won’t have to worry about ensuring that you don’t miss anyone out from your newsletter mailing list again.
  • Treasures, Talents, Time: Have a project that needs more than support? You can source for resources and volunteers on agathos as well.

agathos seeks to rally Christians towards serving the least, the last, and the lost. Join us on this journey as we combine heart and tech to build and nurture a community that is always ready to serve the King and His family here on earth!
